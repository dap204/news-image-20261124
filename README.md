Entry for the make.fun bounty
[news-image-20261124](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124):
sum up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image.

A flat, code-drawn newspaper front page, 1024×1024 PNG. The picture is
drawn by `draw.py` (Python + Pillow, system DejaVu fonts, no network, no
external assets); its output travels in this repository as `entry.b64`
(base64, 4000-character lines) so the repo stays plain text end to end.
`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) decodes `entry.b64`, checks the
PNG magic and IEND trailer, and writes the bytes to `/output/entry.png`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` locally). Two runs always write identical bytes.

Run it:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Regenerate the picture:

```sh
python3 draw.py   # writes entry.png; re-encode to entry.b64 afterwards
```

## The day's news, as drawn

Every fact was re-verified by web search on the evening of 24 November
2026 (WITA) before drawing:

1. **Huang back at Nvidia; shares soar 31% (lead).** Nvidia's board voted
   unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director,
   eleven days after his resignation and following Trump's intervention;
   shares closed up about 31%, adding roughly $950 billion of market
   value — the largest one-day gain for a US company. (CNN, Investopedia,
   TradingView, NY Post, CEO World)
2. **14 nations back ICJ genocide ruling.** Fourteen countries jointly
   acknowledged the International Court of Justice ruling of 19 November
   that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; the UK formally
   recognised the genocide and sanctioned five senior military figures.
   (AFP reporting via Shafaqna / France 24, UK FCDO reporting)
3. **Medvedev floats preventive nuclear strike.** Dmitry Medvedev's
   article argued a massive conventional attack could make a Russian
   preventive nuclear strike possible; the same day Russia's Foreign
   Ministry told Estonia its actions were "on the verge of triggering
   open war" over the border-crossing closure. (Silent Dossier,
   EU Perspectives, Principia daily summary)
4. **NATO vows to defend space itself.** German media reported Western
   intelligence believes Russia's nuclear-armed satellite "Sputnik-S"
   could launch within about 60 days; NATO chief Mark Rutte said in
   Berlin the alliance's defence pledge covers space; the Kremlin
   dismissed the reports. (Reuters, Fox News, Europe Says)
5. **5.6 quake shakes Nepal near Tibet.** A magnitude 5.6 earthquake
   (Nepal's National Earthquake Monitoring and Research Centre; other
   agencies recorded 5.3) struck at 07:17 on Tuesday near Olangchung
   Gola, Taplejung district, Koshi province, close to the Tibet border;
   felt in Kathmandu; no casualties or major damage reported so far.
   (PTI reporting, HT Syndication, Nature World News)
6. **Sanofi to buy Insmed for $41 billion.** Announced Tuesday: $175 a
   share in cash, the biggest biotech takeover in nearly three years.
   (Reuters, WSJ, PR Newswire)
7. **Also today.** Zelensky confirmed seven Ukrainian sea drones sank
   the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, Libya, and hit the cargo
   ship Angara (CNN, Le Monde); UN chief Guterres called for a global AI
   risk framework within a year (Barbados summit reporting); US states
   sued SpaceX over sexualised images generated by Grok (daily news
   summaries, 24 Nov).

Dropped during verification: the morning concept's COP31 "still deadlocked"
framing (the summit collapsed on 22 Nov with no deal and was already the
23 Nov lead), the unverified Banten factory-fire panel, and the US measles
